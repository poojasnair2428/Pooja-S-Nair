About Me

I am an Electronics and Computer Engineering student at Saintgits College of Engineering, driven by a desire to merge hardware intelligence with software to solve real-world problems.

My primary passion lies in embedded systems, IoT, and assistive technology. I believe that engineering is most powerful when it empowers people—whether that means building an ergonomic seating support system for differently-abled students (Edu-Align) or obstacle-sensing smart glasses for the visually impaired.

With strong foundations in C, Python, Arduino, ESP32, and Tinkercad, I actively turn ideas into functional prototypes. As the Women in Tech Lead at Tinkerhub Saintgits and a Technological Innovator at IIC Saintgits, I thrive in collaborative hackathons, community building, and technical leadership.
